Before you decide

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does my brand receive?

It depends on the level. All levels include your logo and QR code in the presentation and a social media mention before and after the event. Pétalo covers one event, Flor covers our 2026 events and Jardín en flor covers every event through June 2027. The comparison table shows what each level adds.

When do I send my logo and photos?

After you reserve. We will ask only for what your profile needs: your logo, a photo, a short purpose statement and the link for your QR code. We will not ask for your contact details again.

Will I receive a receipt for my sponsorship?

Yes. Once we receive your payment we send a sponsorship receipt in your business name, along with a one-page agreement listing your level, the events included and what each party provides.

Do I have to give something to the guests?

No. What matters is that the community gets to know your business. If you would like to offer something, you decide how: a sample, a discount, a raffle prize or whatever fits your business. Tell us your idea when you reserve.

Is the sponsorship for one event or the whole season?

It depends on the level. Pétalo and the in-kind partnership cover one event. Flor covers our 2026 events (Florecer en Gratitud and Mapa de Sueños). Jardín en flor covers every event through June 2027.

What if I can't attend the event?

Your sponsorship is valid whether or not you attend: your brand still appears in the presentation and on social media. For that reason we do not offer refunds. If Mindful Wellness changes an event date, your sponsorship moves to the new date.

How can I pay?

By card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, securely through Stripe, or by Zelle to Mindful Wellness & Coaching LLC.

Can I talk to Mónica before deciding?

Of course. Message her on WhatsApp and we will answer your questions.
